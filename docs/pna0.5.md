# PNA 0.5 — Tactics & Running Notes

## Grey-Area SEO Tactics

### Built into the site (handled in code)
- **JSON-LD structured data** — `Person` + `LocalBusiness` + `Service` schema on every page. Google surfaces in rich results. Zero Chilean freelance competitors do this.
- **hreflang tags** — ES (`/`) and EN (`/en/`) declared as language alternates. Prevents duplicate content penalty, captures bilingual searches.
- **Semantic HTML** — `<article>`, `<main>`, `<address>` used correctly. Google weights these; WordPress themes rarely do.
- **Core Web Vitals** — vanilla JS/CSS scores better than any WordPress competitor automatically. Performance is a ranking factor.

### External tactics (do manually, not in code)
- **Parasite SEO** — publish LinkedIn articles and Dev.to posts targeting "desarrollador web freelance Chile", "sitio web startups Chile", "landing page pymes Chile". These rank on their own domain authority and funnel back to fcarvajalbrown.cl.
- **Google Business Profile** — register as a local business (free). Appears on Maps + local pack above organic results. Almost no individual Chilean devs do this. Priority: do before first outreach batch.
- **Own-property backlink ring** — fcarvajalbrown.github.io/perport → fcarvajalbrown.cl, Zenodo publication → fcarvajalbrown.cl, LinkedIn → fcarvajalbrown.cl. All crawled, all count.
- **Review velocity** — 5 Google reviews on Business Profile in first month moves local ranking more than most on-page work. Ask each of the first 3 clients to leave one.
